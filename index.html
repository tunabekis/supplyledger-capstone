Project Description

SupplyLedger is a supply tracking platform designed to make organizational supply and material flows transparent, traceable, and verifiable. The system records product, material, and resource movements across the supply chain on a secure infrastructure, creating a shared, verifiable history rather than relying on records maintained by a single entity.

Stakeholders and users can follow inputs entered into the platform, monitor progress throughout supply stages, and review how recorded resources move over time. By providing an auditable structure resistant to unauthorized tampering, SupplyLedger aims to enhance supply flow transparency and streamline overall process control. The project will deliver a working prototype demonstrating this approach; specific architectural and implementation choices will be defined during development.
