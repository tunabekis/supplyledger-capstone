Project Description

GiveTrace is a proposed web platform that aims to make donation activity easier to follow and verify. The project addresses the limited visibility donors may have into how campaign funds are recorded and reported. It will provide a shared, time-ordered history of campaign-related donation and fund-use records, with the goal of improving transparency between donors and campaign organizers.

The team plans to develop a prototype using blockchain technology, with Ethereum or another suitable blockchain platform to be selected after considering the project requirements. Users will be able to view campaign information and its recorded activity. The prototype will use test or simulated data and will not handle real donations. Blockchain records can make submitted information more traceable, but do not by themselves prove that a reported real-world expense is accurate.
